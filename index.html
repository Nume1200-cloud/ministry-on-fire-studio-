Questions

Frequently asked

Who is this for?

Pastors, associate ministers, evangelists, Bible teachers and church administrators who prepare messages and lessons week after week.

Which Bible translations does it use?

The King James Version (KJV) and the World English Bible (WEB). Both are public domain, so they can be quoted freely.

Will it write my sermon for me?

It gives you a strong first draft and structure. The message is still yours to pray over, study and deliver in your own voice.
